Who We Are

Our Mission, Vision & Values

TTC NYAMATA is committed to developing knowledgeable, responsible, ethical and innovative educators who can contribute positively to the development of Rwanda and beyond.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality teacher education that develops professional knowledge, practical skills, ethical responsibility and a commitment to lifelong learning.

We prepare future educators to respond effectively to the changing needs of learners, schools and communities.

🌍

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in teacher education, innovation, leadership and professional development, producing competent educators who make a positive impact on society.

Education with Purpose

We believe that effective teacher education goes beyond academic knowledge. It develops character, practical competence, creativity, leadership and responsibility.

Our learning environment encourages future educators to think critically, collaborate, use technology responsibly and remain committed to continuous improvement.
