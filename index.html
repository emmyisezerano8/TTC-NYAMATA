About TTC NYAMATA

Education that creates meaningful impact.

TTC NYAMATA is dedicated to preparing future educators with the knowledge, professional skills, values and innovative mindset needed to make a positive difference in schools and communities.

Our learning environment encourages academic excellence, creativity, collaboration, discipline and responsible leadership.
